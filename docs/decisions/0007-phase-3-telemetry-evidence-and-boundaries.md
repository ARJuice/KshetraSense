# Decision 0007: Evidence and safety boundaries for Phase 3 telemetry

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-10-14

## Context

The original Phase 3 draft described the replay dataset as a 2022 dataset with three complete sensor nodes, calibrated volumetric water content (VWC), battery voltage, and enough site data to open valves using a fixed FAO-56 threshold. Inspection of the cited source and downloaded CSVs shows those claims are not supported. The dataset was collected in Parma, Italy, in summer 2023; it contains one shared environmental sensor, three soil sensors, three water meters, and daily crop indicators. Soil moisture is reported as sensor humidity in `%RH`, not VWC. The dataset has no site field-capacity/wilting-point measurements, root-zone profile, or KshetraSense zone mapping.

The raw public files also contain repeated header rows; soil-sensor battery readings are absent in 99% of records, and the source water-meter column's physical scale cannot be independently verified from its values. A model may learn historical sensor patterns, but none of those issues can be repaired by renaming fields or adding assumed constants.

## Decision

1. **Use the pinned, attributed dataset as a historical benchmark only.** Use Mendeley dataset version 2, DOI [10.17632/35wh56287y.2](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/35wh56287y/2), under its listed CC BY 4.0 terms. Cite Laura Belli et al. and preserve the raw source files. The research paper is Belli et al., *Smart agriculture dataset in a tomato cultivation under different irrigation regimes*, Data in Brief 60 (2025), DOI [10.1016/j.dib.2025.111521](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2025.111521).
2. **Keep source units and meanings intact.** Soil humidity remains `soilHumiditySignalPctRh`; it is never called VWC, converted to a water balance, or compared to field capacity. Soil EC remains µS/cm; temperatures remain °C; battery is percent, never volts. The single air station is shared context. Experimental irrigation lines 1/2/3 are not KshetraSense zones or live nodes. Meter observations remain source-reported values in the source-stated m³ unit; do not derive liters/minute, flow, or applied volume until the meter's scale and semantics are validated.
3. **Forecast only the measured sensor signal.** Train a small two-layer GRU to predict future soil-humidity `%RH` sensor output from the preceding 24 hours of soil humidity, soil temperature, soil EC, shared air temperature, and shared air humidity. Its zero-initialized Dense head predicts a scaled residual over the latest sensor reading (the persistence baseline), so a new/untrained head starts at persistence rather than an arbitrary level. Preserve chronological 70/15/15 train/validation/test partitions, fit scaling only on training data, shuffle windows only within the training partition, compare against persistence, and report measured holdout metrics. A prediction is exploratory, has no calibrated confidence interval, and is not a VWC or irrigation recommendation.
4. **Use a CPU Keras 3 + PyTorch runtime for this Python 3.14 Windows workspace.** TensorFlow's current official installation page lists supported Python through 3.13, while the official PyTorch wheel index publishes Windows CPython 3.14 CPU wheels. Keep the model artifact/version and feature contract explicit so its runtime can be revisited if the deployment environment changes. Keras supports PyTorch as a backend ([Keras setup](https://keras.io/getting_started/), [TensorFlow install matrix](https://www.tensorflow.org/install/pip), [PyTorch wheel index](https://download.pytorch.org/whl/torch/)).
5. **Make FAO-56 MAD advisory-only and require calibrated VWC inputs.** Provide a unit-checked calculator that accepts explicit volumetric water contents (m³/m³), field capacity, wilting point, effective root depth, and depletion fraction. It may return TAW, RAW, and a threshold-crossing advisory. It does not consume this dataset's `%RH` signal, invent site parameters, estimate delivery volume, or operate a valve. The FAO-56 relations are `TAW = 1000(θFC − θWP) Zr` and `RAW = p × TAW`; FAO describes p as crop/ET-dependent and gives tomato ranges, not measurements for this project’s field ([FAO-56 Chapter 8](https://www.fao.org/4/X0490E/x0490e0e.htm)).
6. **Treat Isolation Forest output as a review candidate.** It has no labeled fault ground truth here. Emit an unsupervised outlier flag/score, never `sensor_fault`, fault probability, or confidence. A candidate does not suppress an actuator because no actuator control is implemented.
7. **Keep all playback local and visibly synthetic-as-a-replay.** Expose the source, historical period, line treatment, missingness, and interpolation status. Never claim that playback is live, attached to the user's farm, physically colocated with a Soil Zoning field, or connected to a LoRa gateway.

## Consequences

- The Phase 3 demonstration can honestly show historical research observations, a trained temporal model, anomaly candidates, and an independently testable FAO-56 calculation without implying deployment readiness.
- Operational irrigation advice requires a later measured/calibrated field profile, VWC-capable sensors, crop-stage/ET context, validated water-meter scaling, and explicit human approval. Physical actuation additionally requires a hardware safety design and cannot be added by this replay service.
- Model quality must be judged from the recorded temporal holdout results and compared with persistence; no target accuracy is promised in advance. The current artifact and operational-use decision are recorded in [Decision 0008](0008-gru-holdout-quality-and-use.md).
- Phase 4 must label these as historical research lines, use `%RH`/µS/cm/°C/source-reported units, and remove the old VWC, fixed-threshold, valve, flow-rate, and live-gateway mockups.
